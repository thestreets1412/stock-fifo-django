## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| OS/systemd → app process | systemd owns process lifecycle (start/stop/restart/boot) and the execution identity (non-root User=) and filesystem scope (ProtectSystem/ReadWritePaths). A compromise of the app process is contained by these OS-level controls. |
| loopback → app | Confirmed live on the Pi (checkpoint step 6): the service is reachable only on 127.0.0.1, not from other LAN devices. |

## STRIDE Threat Register (ASVS L1; block on high)

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-02-02 | Elevation of Privilege | systemd User= (gunicorn.service) | high | mitigate | Non-root `User=`/`Group=`; verified structurally (User= present, `User=root` absent) and confirmed live on the Pi (checkpoint step 7, `ps` shows non-root owner). A compromised worker cannot gain root. |
